About us

A local team that takes your safety personally.

Unique Automation CCTV started in 2014 with one van and a simple promise: install it right, and be there when it breaks.

Our story

From one van to thousands of secured sites.

We began as two technicians fixing poorly installed CCTV systems for local shopkeepers. Most problems came down to the same things — cameras in the wrong place, cheap cabling, and no one picking up the phone after installation.

So we built a company around doing those basics properly. Today our team of 40+ engineers and technicians designs and maintains systems for homes, retail chains, hospitals, schools, housing societies and factories.

Technology has moved from grainy analog to 4K AI cameras, but the promise hasn't changed: install it right, and be there when you need us.
